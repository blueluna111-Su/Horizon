## [諾丁漢 NHS 產科醜聞致 520 起傷害/死亡，引發公眾調查呼籲](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/jun/24/horrific-maternity-care-failings-at-nottingham-nhs-trust-prompt-calls-for-public-inquiry) ⭐️ 10.0/10
