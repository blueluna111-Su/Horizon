## [Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24597) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The research paper "Qwen-AgentWorld" introduces Qwen-AgentWorld-35B-A3B and Qwen-AgentWorld-397B-A17B, the first language world models capable of simulating agentic environments across seven domains via long chain-of-thought reasoning. This novel approach aims to equip general AI agents with a better understanding of future states and consequences, addressing a key limitation of current LLM-based agents. This development is significant because it tackles a critical weakness of existing LLM-based agents, which often lack awareness of action consequences and struggle with maintaining workflow state. By providing agents with a native world model, Qwen-AgentWorld could lead to more robust, reliable, and autonomous AI agents capable of complex planning and decision-making in diverse real-world scenarios. Qwen-AgentWorld is trained through a three-stage pipeline—CPT injects environment knowledge, SFT activates next-state-prediction reasoning, and RL sharpens simulation fidelity—on over 10 million real-world interaction trajectories. Unlike prior approaches, it treats environment modeling as a native training objective from the CPT stage onward, covering domains like MCP, Search, Terminal, SWE, Android, Web, and OS within a single model.

hackernews · ilreb · Jun 24, 02:21 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48654351)

**Background**: In artificial intelligence, a "world model" is a machine learning system that constructs an internal representation of an environment and predicts how that environment will change over time in response to actions. This internal model allows AI agents to reason about potential future states and plan their actions more effectively without needing to constantly interact with the real environment. Language World Models specifically integrate this concept with large language models, enabling them to simulate complex environments using linguistic representations and chain-of-thought reasoning.
